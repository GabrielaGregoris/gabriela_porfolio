Hopeless Romantic Books

A comprehensive redesign and optimization of the Hopeless Romantic Books Shopify storefront. As a Web Design Intern, I worked to bridge the gap between the store’s curated physical atmosphere in Toronto and its digital presence, focusing on user-centered layouts, brand storytelling, and improved e-commerce functionality.

  • Role: Web Design Intern
  • Tools: Shopify Theme Editing, Figma
  • Duration: Winter 2026 (Jan – April)
  • Optimize Journey: Improving navigation and layout flow.
  • Enhance Branding: Implementing the "Fall in Love With Your Next Read" banner and trope taglines.
  • Physical-to-Digital: Creating the "Staff Picks Wall" as an interactive digital feature.

The Narrative

The Digital Staff Picks Wall

Translating the physical bookstore experience into digital staff profiles with book carousels. The goal was to take an old, static list and transform it into a new, interactive layout that allowed users to connect personally with staff recommendations.

Branding & Direct Engagement

Focusing on the 'Fall in Love With Your Next Read' banner and the implementation of Trope Taglines to improve user connection. We compared the original landing page vs the newly branded redesign to ensure consistency and improve conversion.

Strategic Site Updates

Detailing the addition of the store notification bar and the new 'Store Locations' pop-up. Using responsive mobile improvements, tested vigorously on various devices, greatly improved the overall accessibility of the store.
