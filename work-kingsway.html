Kingsway Movie Theater – Website Redesign

This website redesign for Kingsway Movie Theater focuses on modernizing the digital experience while preserving a classic, cinematic atmosphere. The goal of the project was to create a more accessible and intuitive website that improves usability, visual hierarchy, and responsiveness across all devices, including desktop and mobile. The redesign makes it easier for users to browse films, view showtimes, and engage with the theater’s brand online.

Tools Used: Figma
Team Project: Collaborated with Mirae Lee & Sophia Sengfah
